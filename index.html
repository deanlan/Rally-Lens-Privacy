RallyLens Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

RallyLens is a tennis practice recorder. You film your practice, and the app finds your shots and helps you review them. Everything happens on your iPhone.

What we collect

Only what you send us in a problem report. Your practice videos, poses and results never leave your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and we never sell or share data. The only thing that reaches us is a problem report you choose to send (see "Reporting a problem" below).

What stays on your iPhone

  • Videos and sound you record with the camera and microphone.
  • Body pose data and shot measurements that the app works out from your videos, such as when a shot happens and where your joints are.
  • Your settings, such as video quality, lens, and what you are practicing.
  • Crash and performance reports that iOS gives the app after a crash. They stay on your iPhone unless you choose to export and send them yourself.

This data is stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Practice videos are left out of iCloud and iTunes backups because of their size. Deleting a session, or deleting the app, removes its data for good, so save clips you want to keep to Photos first.

Permissions

  • Camera: to record your practice.
  • Microphone (optional): to hear the ball being hit, so the app can confirm shots and count rallies. If you say no, the app still records video.
  • Photos (optional): to list the videos in your library so you can pick practice sessions to import, and to save clips you choose to keep. Videos you import are copied into the app and analyzed on your iPhone; nothing in your library is uploaded or changed. You can allow all photos, only selected ones, or none. With no access, you can still pick videos one by one in the system picker.
  • Files: when you import a video from the Files app, the app only gets the files you pick.

Reporting a problem

If you tap Report a problem and press Send, what you wrote is sent to us through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), together with the app version, your iPhone model, iOS version and language, and a report file: your Right / Wrong answers on the motion analysis and the crash records. It contains no practice videos and no pose data, unless you add screenshots or a screen recording yourself. It is not linked to your name or Apple ID email; we only see what is in the report. If your iPhone isn't signed in to iCloud, the app opens an email to us instead, with the same content. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. We use these reports only to fix problems, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Sharing

When you share a clip or picture, you choose where it goes using the standard iOS share sheet. What happens after that is up to the app or person you share it with.

Children

RallyLens does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your iPhone (for example, optional AI feedback), we will update this policy before that version is released, and the app will ask you first.

Contact

RallyLens 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

RallyLens 是网球练习录像工具：你录下练习，App 找出每一拍，帮你回看复盘。所有处理都在你的 iPhone 上完成。

我们收集什么

只收你主动发来的问题报告。练习录像、姿态和分析结果都不会离开你的 iPhone。没有账号、没有广告、没有统计分析、没有跟踪，也不出售、不共享任何数据。唯一会发到我们这里的，是你自己在“报告问题”里点了发送的内容（见下文“报告问题”）。

哪些数据保存在你的 iPhone 上

  • 用相机和麦克风录下的视频和声音。
  • App 从视频里算出的身体姿态和击球数据，比如每一拍的时刻、各个关节的位置。
  • 你的设置，比如画质、镜头、这次练什么。
  • App 崩溃后 iOS 交给 App 的崩溃和性能记录。只保存在本机，除非你自己选择导出并发送。

这些数据存在 App 自己的存储空间里。训练录像体积大，不进入 iCloud 和电脑备份。删除一次训练或删除 App，相应的数据会彻底删除，想留的片段请先存到相册。

权限

  • 相机：录制你的练习。
  • 麦克风（可选）：听击球声，用来确认击球、计算回合。不允许也能照常录像。
  • 相册（可选）：列出相册里的视频，方便你挑练习录像来导入；也用来把你想保留的片段存进相册。导入的视频会复制到 App 里、在 iPhone 上分析，相册里的内容不会上传，也不会被修改。你可以允许全部照片、只允许部分照片，或者不允许；不允许时，仍然可以在系统选择器里一个一个地挑视频。
  • 文件：从“文件”App 导入视频时，App 只拿到你选的那几个文件。

报告问题

在“报告问题”里点“发送”后，你写的内容会通过苹果的 iCloud（CloudKit）发给我们，同时附上 App 版本、iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、语言，以及一个报告文件：你对动作分析点过的“准 / 不准”汇总和崩溃记录。不含练习录像和姿态数据，除非你自己附上截图或录屏。这些内容不关联你的姓名或 Apple ID 邮箱，我们只看得到报告里的内容。手机没登录 iCloud 时，App 改为打开一封发给我们的邮件，内容相同。只有你点了发送才会发出。我们只用这些反馈修复问题，用不到时就删除。

分享

分享片段或图片时，由你通过 iOS 系统的分享菜单决定发到哪里。之后的处理由你分享到的 App 或对方决定。

儿童

RallyLens 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更

如果以后的版本加入任何会把数据发出 iPhone 的功能（比如可选的 AI 点评），我们会在该版本发布前更新本政策，并且 App 会先征得你的同意。
