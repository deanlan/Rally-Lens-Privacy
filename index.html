RallyLens Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

RallyLens is a tennis practice recorder. You film your practice, and the app finds your shots and helps you review them. Everything happens on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. RallyLens does not collect, upload, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app does not connect to our servers.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Videos and sound you record with the camera and microphone.
  • Body pose data and shot measurements that the app works out from your videos, such as when a shot happens and where your joints are.
  • Your settings, such as video quality, lens, and what you are practicing.
  • Crash and performance reports that iOS gives the app after a crash. They stay on your iPhone unless you choose to export and send them yourself.

This data is stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Practice videos are left out of iCloud and iTunes backups because of their size. Deleting a session, or deleting the app, removes its data for good, so save clips you want to keep to Photos first.

Permissions

  • Camera: to record your practice.
  • Microphone (optional): to hear the ball being hit, so the app can confirm shots and count rallies. If you say no, the app still records video.
  • Add to Photos (optional): only when you choose to save a clip to your photo library. The app cannot read your photo library. When you import a video, you pick it yourself in the system picker, and the app only gets that one video.

Sharing

When you share a clip or picture, you choose where it goes using the standard iOS share sheet. What happens after that is up to the app or person you share it with.

Children

RallyLens does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your iPhone (for example, optional AI feedback), we will update this policy before that version is released, and the app will ask you first.

RallyLens 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日

RallyLens 是网球练习录像工具：你录下练习，App 找出每一拍，帮你回看复盘。所有处理都在你的 iPhone 上完成。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。RallyLens 不收集、不上传、不出售、不共享任何个人数据。没有账号、没有广告、没有统计分析、没有跟踪。App 不连接我们的服务器。

哪些数据保存在你的 iPhone 上

  • 用相机和麦克风录下的视频和声音。
  • App 从视频里算出的身体姿态和击球数据，比如每一拍的时刻、各个关节的位置。
  • 你的设置，比如画质、镜头、这次练什么。
  • App 崩溃后 iOS 交给 App 的崩溃和性能记录。只保存在本机，除非你自己选择导出并发送。

这些数据存在 App 自己的存储空间里。训练录像体积大，不进入 iCloud 和电脑备份。删除一次训练或删除 App，相应的数据会彻底删除，想留的片段请先存到相册。

权限

  • 相机：录制你的练习。
  • 麦克风（可选）：听击球声，用来确认击球、计算回合。不允许也能照常录像。
  • 添加到相册（可选）：只在你选择把片段存到相册时使用。App 读不到你的相册；导入视频时由你在系统选择器里挑，App 只拿到你选的那一段。

分享

分享片段或图片时，由你通过 iOS 系统的分享菜单决定发到哪里。之后的处理由你分享到的 App 或对方决定。

儿童

RallyLens 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更

如果以后的版本加入任何会把数据发出 iPhone 的功能（比如可选的 AI 点评），我们会在该版本发布前更新本政策，并且 App 会先征得你的同意。
